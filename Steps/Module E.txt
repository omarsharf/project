Module E — AI Agent

This is the most important special requirement.

The AI should have tools.

For example:

get_delivery_status()
get_pending_deliveries()
get_delayed_deliveries()
get_available_drivers()
assign_delivery()
update_delivery_status()

The AI could receive:

"Show me all delayed deliveries."

and choose:

get_delayed_deliveries()

Or:

"Reassign shipment 105 to an available driver."

The flow becomes:

User
 ↓
AI
 ↓
assign_delivery(...)
 ↓
Django validates user permission
 ↓
Django validates shipment + driver
 ↓
Database updated
 ↓
Result → AI
 ↓
User

The specification specifically says AI tools need identity binding, permission checks, parameter validation, and confirmation for destructive actions.
